TECHNOVANZA '26: Official Rulebook
Anjalai Ammal Mahalingam Engineering College
Kovilvenni, near Needamangalam, Thiruvarur
COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING
Welcome to TECHNOVANZA '26, the premier technical symposium of AAMEC, Thiruvarur. This
rulebook outlines the guidelines, fee structures, and event-specific regulations to ensure a fair and
competitive environment.
II. Technical Events
1. WebNova
Mode: Individual
Frontend Web Development
Technologies Allowed
• HTML – Structure
• CSS – Styling & Design
• JavaScript – Functionality & Responsiveness
• Bootstrap and Tailwind CSS are allowed
Rules
• Mobile phones and AI tools are strictly prohibited during the competition. Any participant found using
them will be disqualified immediately.
• The use of browser extensions, libraries, frameworks, templates, or pre-built code is not allowed,
except Bootstrap and Tailwind CSS.
• Participants must develop the website only using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
• The website must be completed within the allotted time.
Evaluation Criteria
• Creativity & UI/UX Design
• Responsive Design (Mobile, Tablet & Desktop)
• Implementation of HTML, CSS & JavaScript
• Code Quality & Functionality
Competition Details
• Time Limit: 40 Minutes
• Development Tool: Visual Studio Code (VS Code) Only
2. TechTalks
Eligibility
• Open topic – Participants may choose any technical topic of their interest.
• Team participation only – 2 members per team.
• Selection Process: Not all teams will be allowed to present. Submission of PPT is mandatory for
screening.
Presentation Submission & Selection
• The presentation (PPT) must be submitted 5 days before the event using the Google Drive link
provided by the organizers.
• The organizing team will review all submissions.
• Only teams who receive a confirmation email will be allowed to present on the event day.
• Late submissions will not be accepted. Failure to submit before the deadline will result in
disqualification.
PowerPoint Guidelines
Presentation should contain 10–12 slides only and must include:
• Title Slide – Team Details & Topic
• Problem Statement
• Abstract
• Proposed Solution
• Content Slides – Methodology, Results, etc.
• Conclusion
• Thank You Slide
• Use clear fonts, proper formatting, and relevant visuals.
• The presentation must be free from plagiarism.
Prototype Requirement
• Each team must present a working prototype/model/demo of their project on the day of presentation.
• Teams without a prototype will be awarded reduced marks.
Time Limit
• Presentation: 7 Minutes
• Question & Answer (Viva): 3 Minutes
• Total Time: 10 Minutes
• Exceeding the allotted time may lead to deduction of marks.
Evaluation Criteria
• Problem Statement & Relevance
• Technical Knowledge & Solution
• Innovation & Creativity
• Quality of Prototype/Demo
• Content Quality & Presentation Skills
• Response to Judges' Questions
• Time Management
General Rules
• The decision of the judges will be final and binding.
• Participants must report to the venue 30 minutes before the event starts.
• Bring a backup copy of your presentation and prototype files on a pen drive.
• Any form of plagiarism or inappropriate content will lead to immediate disqualification.

4. CodeFusion
Overview
The Coding Contest (CC) consists of two rounds designed to evaluate participants' theoretical
knowledge, debugging ability, and programming skills.
• Total Participants: 30
• Total Time Slots Allocated: 3
• Duration of Each Slot: 1 Hour
Participant Distribution
To avoid scheduling conflicts with other symposium events, the 30 participants will be divided into two
batches of 15 participants each.
Slot 1
• 15 participants attend the Coding Contest.
• The remaining 15 participants attend their other registered events.
Slot 2
• The remaining 15 participants attend the Coding Contest.
• Participants from Slot 1 are free to attend their other events.
This arrangement ensures that participants can take part in multiple symposium events without
overlap.
Round 1 – Technical Quiz (Paper & Pen)
• Mode: Offline (Paper & Pen)
• Duration: Conducted within each respective 1-hour slot.
Topics
Questions will be based on core Computer Science subjects from the Anna University syllabus, including:
• Programming/Coding Fundamentals
• Operating Systems (OS)
• Object-Oriented Software Engineering (OOSE)
• Database Management Systems (DBMS)
• Other core Computer Science subjects
Selection
Each batch consists of 15 participants. From each batch, the top 7 participants will qualify for Round 2.
• Slot 1 → 7 Qualified
• Slot 2 → 7 Qualified
• Total Qualified for Round 2: 14 Participants
Round 2 – Coding Challenge (HackerRank)
• Mode: HackerRank
• Participants: 14 Qualified Participants
• Duration: 1 Hour
Section A – Debugging
• Participants will be given a program containing errors.
• They must identify and correct the errors.
• The debugging code will be available in C, Python, and Java.
• This section carries comparatively lower weightage than the coding section.
Section B – Coding
• Participants will receive a programming problem statement.
• Sample and hidden test cases will be used for evaluation.
• Participants must write a correct and efficient solution.
• The submitted program should pass all test cases.
• Participants may choose to solve the problem using C, Python, or Java.
• The coding problems will be of Medium and Hard difficulty levels.
The coding section will have higher priority in the final evaluation.
Event Flow
Slot 1 15 Participants → Round 1 (Technical Quiz) → Top 7 qualify for Round 2
Slot 2 15 Participants → Round 1 (Technical Quiz) → Top 7 qualify for Round 2
Final Round 14 Qualified Participants → Round 2 on HackerRank (Debugging + Coding)
The final winners will be determined based on their overall performance in Round 2, with greater
emphasis on the coding challenge.
III. Non-Technical Events
1. Fun Feast
The event consists of 8 fun games.
• Each game has a time limit of 2 minutes.
• Participants must follow the game rules and the judges' decisions.
• The team with the highest overall score will be declared the winner.
2. Brain Battle
Mode: Individual (Team participation is not allowed)
Questions will cover:
• Current Affairs
• Cinema
• General Knowledge
• Memes
• Mobile phones, smartwatches, notes, or any other external help are not allowed.
• A buzzer round will be conducted on a first-come, first-served basis. Each question must be answered
within 30 seconds.
• Once an answer is given, it cannot be changed.
• The Quiz Master's decision is final.
• In case of a tie, a tie-breaker round will be conducted to decide the winner.
3. Nexus
• Participants will be shown a set of images.
• Once the images are displayed, participants can press the buzzer to answer.
• The first participant to press the buzzer gets the first chance to answer.
• If the first participant answers incorrectly, the chance will pass to the next participant based on the
order in which the buzzers were pressed.
• The team with the highest number of correct answers will be declared the winner.
• There is no negative marking.
4. Checkmate Challenge
• Follow all standard chess rules.
• White always makes the first move.
• The touch-move rule applies. If you touch a piece, you must move it (if it is a legal move).
• Mobile phones, cheating, or any outside assistance are strictly prohibited.
• The player who checkmates the opponent within one hour will be declared the winner.
• If neither player achieves checkmate within one hour, the winner will be decided according to the
tournament rules (for example, based on the board position or by a tie-break method).
IV. Code of Conduct
• All participants must carry their college ID cards.
• The decision of the event coordinators and judges is final.
• If any malpractices are found, the participant(s) will be disqualified from the event.
