TECHNOVANZA '26: Official Rulebook
Anjalai Ammal Mahalingam Engineering College
Kovilvenni, near Needamangalam, Thiruvarur
Computer Science and Engineering

Welcome to TECHNOVANZA '26, the premier technical symposium of AAMEC, Thiruvarur.
This rulebook outlines the guidelines and event-specific regulations to ensure a fair and competitive environment.

I. Technical Events

1. WebNova
Mode: Individual
Frontend Web Development

Technologies Allowed
- HTML - Structure
- CSS - Styling & Design
- JavaScript - Functionality & Responsiveness
- Bootstrap and Tailwind CSS are allowed

Rules
- Mobile phones and AI tools are strictly prohibited during the competition.
- Any participant found using mobile phones or AI tools will be disqualified immediately.
- The use of browser extensions, libraries, frameworks, templates, or pre-built code is not allowed, except Bootstrap and Tailwind CSS.
- Participants must develop the website only using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- The website must be completed within the allotted time.

Evaluation Criteria
- Creativity & UI/UX Design
- Responsive Design for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Implementation of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Code Quality & Functionality

2. TechTalks
Mode: Team, exactly 2 members

Eligibility & Domains
- Team: Exactly 2 members per team.
- Domains: AI, ML, Digital Twin Technology, Cyber Security, Cloud & DevOps, IoT & Edge Computing, Blockchain, Full Stack Development, Data Science, Quantum Computing, or any other relevant technical domain chosen by the participants.

Submission & Screening
- Mandatory Submission: Submit the paper in IEEE format 5 days before the event using the provided Google Drive link for selection.
- Drive link: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1WmAYChPlnLMkOuUfAd_x5fItuMN9VG9d?usp=sharing
- Selection: Only pre-screened teams receiving a confirmation email may present.
- No late submissions are allowed.

PPT Format: 10-12 slides only
- Title Slide with team details and topic
- Problem Statement
- Abstract
- Existing System
- Proposed Solution
- Content Slides covering methodology, architecture, and results
- Conclusion

Prototype & Time Limits
- Mandatory Prototype: Teams must bring a working prototype or demo.
- Teams without a demo face severe mark deductions.
- Time: 7 minutes presentation + 3 minutes Q&A = 10 minutes total.
- Exceeding the time limit reduces marks.

Evaluation Criteria
- Problem Statement & Relevance
- Technical Depth & Innovation
- Prototype Quality & Execution
- Presentation Skills & Slide Quality
- Q&A Response
- Time Management

Key Rules
- Report 10 minutes before the event.
- Bring PPT and demo backups on a USB drive.
- Plagiarism results in instant disqualification.

3. Prompt Maestro
Mode: Individual

Round 1: Vision 2 Prompt - AI Prompt Engineering
Participants will be given one or more AI-based tasks and must write effective prompts to generate accurate, creative, and high-quality outputs.
Tasks may include image generation, text generation, reasoning, transformation, summarization, and other prompt engineering challenges.

Rules
- Individual participation only.
- A common task or scenario will be provided to all participants.
- Participants may use any prompt engineering technique to improve their prompts.
- Manual editing of the AI-generated output is not allowed unless specified by the organizers.
- The final prompt and AI-generated output must be submitted before the deadline.

Evaluation Criteria
- Prompt Quality & Clarity
- Creativity & Innovation
- Accuracy & Relevance
- Prompt Optimization & Effectiveness
- Quality of AI-Generated Output

Round 2: Dream 2 DOM - AI-Powered Web Development
Participants will be given a problem statement and must write effective prompts to generate a complete, responsive web page using AI.

Rules
- Individual participation only.
- A common problem statement will be provided.
- Manual coding is strictly prohibited.
- Participants must generate the website using Bolt.ai or any AI tool specified by the organizers.
- Participants may refine their prompts multiple times within the allotted time.
- The final prompt and generated web page must be submitted before the deadline.

Evaluation Criteria
- Prompt Quality & Clarity
- UI/UX Design & Creativity
- Responsive Web Design
- Functionality & Completeness
- Adherence to the Given Requirements

4. CodeFusion
Mode: Individual

Participation & Identification
- Participants must report 10 minutes before their allotted slot with a valid Student ID and registration confirmation.
- Participants without valid identification will not be allowed to participate.

Round 1 - Technical Quiz
- The quiz will be conducted in paper and pen mode.
- Participants must bring their own pen and submit their answers within the allotted time.

Round 2 - Coding Challenge
- The second round will be conducted on HackerRank for the qualified participants.
- It will consist of debugging and coding challenges using C, Python, or Java.

Use of Systems
- For Round 2, participants must use only the computer systems provided by the organizers.
- Personal laptops or other personal systems are strictly not allowed.

Prohibited Resources
- The use of mobile phones, AI tools, websites, calculators, smart watches, books, notes, or any other external assistance is strictly prohibited in both rounds.

No Malpractice
- Copying, sharing answers or code, discussing solutions, collaboration, plagiarism, or any form of cheating is strictly prohibited.
- Any such activity will result in immediate disqualification.

Submission & Time
- Participants must submit their answer sheet or code before the allotted time ends.
- Submission time may be considered for tie-breaking.

Tie-Breaking
Round 1:
- Higher correct answers
- Better accuracy
- Earlier submission
- Oral tie-break, if required

Round 2:
- Higher coding score
- More hidden test cases passed
- Earlier submission
- Additional tie-break problem, if required

Discipline & Technical Issues
- Participants must follow the instructions of the organizers and proctors.
- Any technical issue during Round 2 must be reported immediately to the proctor.
- Participants must maintain proper discipline throughout the event.

Final Decision
- Complaints or result-related queries must be submitted within 30 minutes of the announcement of results.
- The organizers reserve the right to modify the rules or schedule when necessary, and any changes will be communicated to the participants.

II. Non-Technical Events

1. Fun Feast
Mode: Team, 2 members
- The event consists of 8 fun games.
- Each game has a time limit of 2 minutes.
- Participants must follow the game rules and the judges' decisions.
- The team with the highest overall score will be declared the winner.

2. Brain Battle
Mode: Individual
Team participation is not allowed.

Questions will cover:
- Current Affairs
- Cinema
- General Knowledge
- Memes

Rules
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, notes, or any other external help are not allowed.
- A buzzer round will be conducted on a first-come, first-served basis.
- Each question must be answered within 30 seconds.
- Once an answer is given, it cannot be changed.
- The Quiz Master's decision is final.
- In case of a tie, a tie-breaker round will be conducted to decide the winner.

3. Nexus
Mode: Team, 2 members
- Participants will be shown a set of images.
- Once the images are displayed, participants can press the buzzer to answer.
- The first participant to press the buzzer gets the first chance to answer.
- If the first participant answers incorrectly, the chance will pass to the next participant based on the order in which the buzzers were pressed.
- The team with the highest number of correct answers will be declared the winner.
- There is no negative marking.

4. Checkmate Challenge
Mode: Individual
- Follow all standard chess rules.
- White always makes the first move.
- The touch-move rule applies. If you touch a piece, you must move it if it is a legal move.
- Mobile phones, cheating, or any outside assistance are strictly prohibited.
- The player who checkmates the opponent within one hour will be declared the winner.
- If neither player achieves checkmate within one hour, the winner will be decided according to the tournament rules, for example based on board position or by a tie-break method.

III. Code of Conduct
- All participants must carry their college ID cards.
- The decision of the event coordinators and judges is final.
- If any malpractices are found, the participant or participants will be disqualified from the event.
